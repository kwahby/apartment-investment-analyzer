User Guide

Apartment Investment Analyzer

A fast, private tool that tells you whether a German rental apartment is a good investment. Enter a listing's numbers and get an instant buy / caution / avoid verdict, full cash-flow and financing analysis, and a multi-year after-tax projection — all in plain language.

Get started Tour the app
Not financial or tax advice. This is a decision-support tool. Every figure is an estimate — verify against professional advice and current market data before making any decision.

What it does

One screen turns a property listing into a clear, defensible investment read.

Instant verdict

A Buy / Caution / Avoid score built from yield, cash flow, price vs. the local market, and location.

Live analysis

Yields, monthly cash flow, financing and a multi-year projection recalculate instantly as you type.

Built for Germany

Annuity loans, Zinsbindung + follow-up financing, AfA depreciation, Spekulationssteuer and the 10-year rule.

Who it's for

Anyone weighing up a rental flat — from a first-time buyer who wants plain-language guidance, to an experienced investor who wants the numbers to hold up. No spreadsheets, no accounts, nothing to install.

Getting started in three steps

From a property advert to a verdict in a couple of minutes.

Enter the listing

Type in the purchase price, size (m²), and expected cold rent from the advert. Add the balcony area, Hausgeld (HOA fee) and build year if you have them. Every field has a small ⓘ hint explaining it in plain words.

Set your financing

Enter your down payment, interest rate, the fixed-interest period (Zinsbindung) and your repayment rate (Tilgung %). Pick whether to assume one rate for the whole loan, or to refinance the remainder afterwards.

Read the verdict & explore

The banner at the top gives a Buy / Caution / Avoid call. Then browse the tabs for cash flow, financing, and the long-term projection. Save the deal, or export a one-page PDF to share.

Don't know your local averages? Type a postal code or city into the market-benchmark field and the app fills in reference €/m² and rent figures for you. You can override any of them.

A tour of the tabs

The analysis is organised into tabs, each answering one question.

Summary

The plain-English headline: the verdict, what it would cost you each month, and the one or two things that make or break the deal. Start here.

Key Metrics

The investor's dashboard: gross & net yield, price multiple (Kaufpreisfaktor), cash-on-cash return, and a market benchmark comparing the listing's €/m² and rent/m² to the local average.

Cash flow

Where the money goes each month — rent in, costs and loan out. Includes a yearly cash-flow chart that marks the year the deal turns positive, and an income-vs-outflow chart showing exactly why each year is red or green.

Financing

How your mortgage really works. A jargon-free explainer of the interest + Tilgung payment, a chart of the loan shrinking over time (with the effect of any extra repayments), and the full year-by-year payment schedule.

Projection

The long game: property appreciation, rent growth, a sale at the end of your holding period, IRR and money multiple — plus a full after-tax view and a property-vs-ETF comparison of the same cash.

Scenarios

A one-glance stress test: Cautious, Base and Optimistic versions of the deal side by side, so you can see how sensitive the outcome is to your assumptions.

Compare

Save several listings and line them up in one table, with the best value flagged for each metric — perfect for choosing between a shortlist of flats.

Key terms, in plain English

The handful of concepts worth knowing before you read the numbers.

Cold rent Kaltmiete
The rent for the flat itself, without heating and utilities. This is your income; the extras just pass through to the tenant.
Gross & net yield Brutto-/Nettomietrendite
Yearly rent as a percentage of the price. Gross ignores costs; net subtracts running costs — the more honest number.
Price multiple Kaufpreisfaktor
How many years of rent equal the purchase price. Lower is cheaper. A quick way to gauge if a flat is over-priced.
Cash flow
What's left each month after rent pays the running costs and the loan. Negative means you top it up; positive means it pays you.
Repayment rate Tilgung
How fast you pay the loan down. It's added to the interest rate to form one fixed monthly payment — it isn't paid on top. Higher Tilgung = debt-free sooner. It builds your equity, so it isn't "lost" money.
Fixed-interest period Zinsbindung
How long the bank guarantees your interest rate — commonly 5, 10, 15 or 20 years. After it ends, the rate can change.
Remaining debt Restschuld
What you still owe when the fixed period ends. You either keep paying at the same rate, or refinance it (Anschlussfinanzierung).
Closing costs Kaufnebenkosten
The extra costs on top of the price: transfer tax, notary, land registry and agent — typically ~9–12% in Germany.
HOA fee Hausgeld
The monthly building fee. Part of it (umlagefähig) can be recharged to the tenant; the rest is your cost.
Depreciation AfA
A yearly tax deduction on the building's value that reduces your taxable rental income (in the after-tax view).
Speculation tax Spekulationssteuer
Tax on the profit if you sell within 10 years. Hold 10+ years and the sale profit is generally tax-free.

Private by design

Your numbers never leave your device.

  • No server, no accounts, no sign-up — the whole app runs in your browser.
  • Everything you enter is stored only in your own browser and is never uploaded.
  • No analytics or tracking. Clearing your browser data (or the in-app reset) removes it.
  • Works offline once loaded — you can even use it while viewing a flat with no signal.
Bottom line: use it to turn any listing into a clear, shareable investment read in minutes — then confirm the assumptions with a professional before you commit.
